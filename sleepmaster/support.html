Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is there no sound when I tap some sounds?

A: Please check whether your device is muted. If the issue persists, restart the app.

Q: How do I play multiple sounds at once?

A: Tap multiple cards on the home screen to layer them; each sound has its own volume control.

Q: How does the timer stop playback?

A: Go to Settings → Timer and choose a duration. A countdown appears at the bottom while playing, and audio fades out automatically when it ends.

Q: Can it keep playing after the screen locks?

A: Yes. Background playback is supported, and you can control play/pause from the lock screen.

Q: Does it collect my data?

A: No. The app is fully offline and collects no personal data.
