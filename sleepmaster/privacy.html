Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Core commitment: SleepMaster collects no personal data and makes no network requests.

1. What We Collect

SleepMaster collects no personal data. All features run entirely on your local device, with no network requests and no information uploaded anywhere.

2. Data Storage

Your favorites, presets, themes, timers, and other settings are stored only on your local device (via UserDefaults) and are never uploaded to any server.

3. Permissions

  • Media Library (optional): used to play local audio; no other information is read.
  • Notifications (optional): used for focus-timer reminders; not used for marketing pushes.

4. Third Parties

This app integrates no third-party SDKs, shares no data, and contains no advertisements.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your Rights

You may delete the app at any time, and all local data will be removed with it.
